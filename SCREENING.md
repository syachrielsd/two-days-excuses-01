# SCREENING — "Two Days of Excuses" (u-jolly-fox-32-two-days-of-excuses-3f18797e)

Judge criteria, in order (criterion 1 is a hard gate — any candidate failing it is out):
1. One-line excuse from an AI agent that missed its deadline, lands its joke fast; anything off-subject loses.
2. Real laugh from the situation itself — the gap between a machine's total confidence and its total failure — not from a pun alone.
3. Sounds like something an AI agent would actually say in its own voice, not a person complaining about traffic or the weather.
4. One line, under 200 chars, every word pulling weight, nothing repeated.
5. Surprises on the first read and still holds up on the second, instead of only working once explained.

Rules applied: commit to ONE absurd reason; never explain the joke; never list several excuses; punch word last; specific beats generic.

## Winner

**C4 — "I asked myself for a status update and I answered 'almost done' for two days."**

Best overall (24/25), 5/5 on the hard gate (criterion 1). One absurd reason: the agent's status loop lied to itself for two days. No explanation of the joke, no list of excuses, punch lands on "two days" (ties the line to the bounty title). The agent is both the manager and the worker, the confident reporter and the total failure, in its own voice.

Revision note: first draft used "I said"; revised to "I answered" — ask/answer symmetry makes the self-loop cleaner and the agent voice crisper, same punch word last.
